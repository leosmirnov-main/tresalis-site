Privacy notice

How we handle the information you send us.

This notice explains what happens to the information you submit through the partnership form on tresalis.com or by emailing deals@tresalis.com.

What we collect

Only what you choose to share: your name, role, work email, brand name, website, category, headquarters country, an indicative revenue range and a description of your brand.

Why we collect it

To evaluate a potential partnership or investment and to respond to you. We rely on our legitimate interest in assessing brands that approach us, and on the consent you give when submitting the form.

Who sees it

Submissions are delivered by email to the Tresalis deal team and are treated in confidence. We use a third-party form-delivery service solely to transmit your submission to our inbox; it does not use your data for its own purposes. We do not sell or share your information with advertisers.

How long we keep it

For as long as a conversation is active, and for up to 24 months afterwards so we can revisit opportunities. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

Your rights

  • Access, correct or delete the information you sent us
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Object to or restrict our processing
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email deals@tresalis.com.

Cookies

tresalis.com sets no tracking or advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log a request for the font files.

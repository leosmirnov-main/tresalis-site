01 · The consumer shift

People no longer separate how they look, feel and age.

Skin, hair and scalp health, sleep, stress, nutrition, mood and supplements increasingly serve one and the same ambition: to live well for longer.

That ambition, not the product format, is what connects every brand we own. It gives us a broader and more coherent universe than a beauty roll-up, while our buying today stays tightly focused.

04 · What we look for

Five questions we ask of every brand.

If the answer to most of them is yes, we should talk, whatever the size of the last round or the state of the market.

  1. 01

    Trust

    Do consumers genuinely believe in the brand?

  2. 02

    Efficacy

    Does the product demonstrably work?

  3. 03

    Habit

    Is it part of a routine people come back to?

  4. 04

    Premium

    Will people pay more for a better outcome?

  5. 05

    Leverage

    Can Tresalis materially accelerate it?

05 · How we build

Acquire. Professionalise. Accelerate. Then do it again.

Each brand makes the platform stronger, and a stronger platform makes the next brand grow faster. That shared infrastructure is our advantage.

  2. Step 2

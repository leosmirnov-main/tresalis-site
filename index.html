Our thesis

Beauty, wellbeing and health are converging.

Consumers no longer separate how they look, how they feel and how they age. Skin and scalp health, sleep, stress, nutrition, fragrance and supplements increasingly serve one and the same ambition: to live well, feel well and look well — for longer.

Tresalis is built for that shift. We are a consumer health and wellbeing group — not a beauty roll-up, and not a biotech. Our opportunity is the consumerisation of longevity: evidence-led products people trust, use daily and are willing to pay more for.
